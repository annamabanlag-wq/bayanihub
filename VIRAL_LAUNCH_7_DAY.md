# BayaniHub — 7-Day Public Launch Sprint

Goal: grow genuine awareness for BayaniHub while protecting donor and campaign trust.

## The hook
**BAYANIHUB — TULONG PARA SA PILIPINO**

A community fundraising platform for Filipinos in need, with campaign review and manual payment verification.

## Day 1 — Founder story
Share why BayaniHub was built and invite NGOs, churches, schools, barangay/community groups and volunteers to review the platform.

Do not claim government or NGO affiliation unless a real partnership exists.

## Day 2 — Campaign story
Feature one real approved campaign with the organizer's permission.
Include:
- what help is needed
- location
- target
- verification status
- direct campaign link

Never present demo/sample amounts as real donations.

## Day 3 — Partner outreach
Send a short personal message to legitimate organizations:
> We built BayaniHub to help Filipino families and communities share verified fundraising needs. We'd appreciate your feedback on our verification process and, if appropriate, your help sharing legitimate campaigns.

## Day 4 — Short-form video
Create 15–30 second clips:
1. “What is BayaniHub?”
2. “How a campaign gets reviewed.”
3. “How to help a Filipino family through a campaign.”

## Day 5 — Community participation
Participate in Filipino community, charity, local-city and volunteer communities. Answer questions first. Share BayaniHub only where promotion is permitted and relevant.

## Day 6 — Sponsor outreach
Approach businesses and supporters that may want legitimate sponsor placement. Sponsorship requests remain pending until admin verification.

## Day 7 — Trust report
Publish a simple weekly transparency post:
- campaigns submitted
- campaigns approved
- donations confirmed
- confirmed platform revenue
- rejected/flagged activity

Use actual database figures only.

## Copy/paste public post
🇵🇭 **BAYANIHUB — TULONG PARA SA PILIPINO**

Need help? Start a fundraising campaign and provide your story and supporting evidence.

Want to help? Discover community campaigns and support a legitimate cause.

BayaniHub: https://bayanihub.vercel.app/

Campaigns are reviewed before going live and payment transactions are subject to manual verification.

## Copy/paste partner message
Hello! We are building BayaniHub, a community fundraising platform for Filipinos in need. We are looking for legitimate NGOs, churches, schools, community groups and volunteers who can review our process and help us improve trust and reach. If your organization is interested, we can share the platform and verification workflow for review.

## Distribution rule
Use public, lawful channels only. Never use fake urgency, fake donors, fake raised amounts, impersonation, spam, compromised sites or illicit/dark-web marketplaces.

## Success definition
Measure real campaigns, verified donations, donor participation and legitimate partnerships — not inflated traffic numbers.
