Send help via GCash

Manual GCash donation • Admin verifies every transfer before campaign totals change

1. Scan the BayaniHub donation QR

Scan with the GCash app. Confirm the recipient name shown by GCash before sending. The QR is the payment destination currently configured for BayaniHub donations.

2. Send your chosen amount

3. Submit proof for verification

Your donation remains pending until an authorized BayaniHub admin verifies the GCash transfer. Pending donations do not increase the public campaign total.

BayaniHub does not deduct a platform fee from campaign donations. After verification, the full donation amount goes to the campaign. BayaniHub is supported separately by voluntary donations and advertising.

Never send money to anyone claiming to represent BayaniHub outside this official payment screen.
