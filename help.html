We will help you reach legitimate assistance.

BayaniHub is a community platform, not a government agency. For urgent medical or crisis needs, also apply directly through official government assistance channels below.

🇵🇭 Senate Public Assistance

Senate Assist accepts online requests for medical and social assistance, including hospital bills, surgery, medicines, burial and transportation assistance.

Open Senate Assist

🤝 DSWD AICS

DSWD's Assistance to Individuals in Crisis Situations can provide medical, funeral, food, transportation, educational and other crisis assistance after assessment.

Open DSWD AICS

🏥 PCSO Medical Assistance

PCSO provides medical assistance through its official Medical Assistance Program. Prepare the required medical and billing documents before applying.

Open PCSO Medical Assistance

📄 Prepare your proof

For medical assistance, official agencies may require IDs, clinical abstracts/medical certificates, hospital statements of account, prescriptions or other supporting documents. Never pay anyone who promises guaranteed government assistance.
