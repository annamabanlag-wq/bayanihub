How BayaniHub works

1. Ask for help

Post a campaign on Start. Add a clear story, location, goal, and at least one proof photo. Admin reviews before it goes public.

2. Support is shared securely

Approved campaigns currently use a temporary manual GCash flow. Donors submit the GCash reference and BayaniHub verifies the transfer before the campaign total changes. Supporters can also share campaigns with people, organizations, and official assistance channels.

3. Admin checks, then releases

We verify that the beneficiary/campaign is genuine and check the donor's payment receipt before a donation is confirmed. BayaniHub does not charge people to request help.

🆓 Free to request help

Creating a campaign and requesting help is free. There is no posting or follow-up service fee.

Rules

  • Honest stories only. Fake campaigns get rejected.
  • There is no donation minimum above ₱1. Any whole-peso amount is welcome on real, approved campaigns.
  • Not a bank, not a government program, not an investment.
  • Share links on Messenger / WhatsApp / Facebook — that is how help spreads.
