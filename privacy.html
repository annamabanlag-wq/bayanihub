Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

1. Who we are

BayaniHub is a community-powered fundraising platform that helps Filipinos raise support for medical needs, education, pet care, family assistance, bereavement, community projects, and similar causes. We are not a bank, not a government agency, and not affiliated with GCash or any government body.

2. Information we collect

  • Campaign information – title, story, goal amount, location, organizer name, contact number.
  • Evidence / proof documents – photos and files (medical abstracts, bills, IDs, vet records, school papers, etc.) that you upload to support your campaign. These are used only for verification by the admin.
  • Donation notifications – name (optional), amount, payment reference number, and the campaign you supported.
  • Technical data – basic browser/device and local storage information used to keep the PWA working on your device and preserve your local dashboard state.

3. How we use your information

  • To review and approve/reject campaigns and evidence.
  • To display approved campaigns publicly so donors can help.
  • To verify payment transactions using the reference numbers you provide.
  • To contact organizers when needed about their campaign.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data or evidence files with third parties for marketing.

4. Evidence & privacy

Uploaded evidence (photos, PDFs, documents) is treated as sensitive. New evidence is stored in a private BayaniHub storage bucket and is available only to authorized BayaniHub admins through temporary access links. Public campaign listings do not expose the evidence files. We recommend you blur or crop highly sensitive personal details (full ID numbers, etc.) before uploading.

5. Payments

BayaniHub is not a bank and does not ask users to send money to a personal account. Donations are temporarily paused while BayaniHub connects a verified business payment channel. BayaniHub will never ask for a donor's PIN, OTP, full account password, or other authentication credentials.

6. Advertising and cookies

BayaniHub may display advertisements from Google AdSense and other advertising partners. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to BayaniHub or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and other sites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Users may also learn about choices for some third-party personalized advertising at aboutads.info.

7. Data storage

BayaniHub uses Supabase for production campaign, donation-verification, sponsorship, and admin records. Access to private operational records is restricted by database row-level security and authorized admin accounts. Some non-sensitive interface state is also kept locally in your browser so the PWA can work smoothly and preserve your local dashboard state. Clearing browser storage removes that local copy, but does not by itself delete records already submitted to BayaniHub.

8. Your rights

  • You may request deletion of your campaign or personal data by contacting the admin.
  • You may ask to see what information we hold about you.
  • You can withdraw a pending campaign before it is approved.

9. Children’s privacy

Campaigns involving minors should be created by a parent, guardian, or authorized adult. We do not knowingly collect personal data directly from children under 13.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page with an updated date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, data requests, or concerns about a campaign, contact the BayaniHub admin through the channels published on the site or the email used for sponsor inquiries.
