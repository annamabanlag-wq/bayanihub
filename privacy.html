Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

1. Who we are

BayaniHub is a community-powered fundraising platform that helps Filipinos raise support for medical needs, education, pet care, family assistance, bereavement, community projects, and similar causes. We are not a bank, not a government agency, and not affiliated with GCash or any government body.

2. Information we collect

  • Campaign information – title, story, goal amount, location, organizer name, contact number.
  • Evidence / proof documents – photos and files (medical abstracts, bills, IDs, vet records, school papers, etc.) that you upload to support your campaign. These are used only for verification by the admin.
  • Donation notifications – name (optional), amount, GCash reference number, and the campaign you supported.
  • Technical data – basic browser/device info stored locally for the app to work offline (localStorage).

3. How we use your information

  • To review and approve/reject campaigns and evidence.
  • To display approved campaigns publicly so donors can help.
  • To verify GCash transfers using the reference numbers you provide.
  • To contact organizers when needed about their campaign.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data or evidence files with third parties for marketing.

4. Evidence & privacy

Uploaded evidence (photos, PDFs, documents) is treated as sensitive. Only the platform admin can view the full files during review. Public campaign pages may show a small preview or simply state that evidence was submitted and reviewed. We recommend you blur or crop highly sensitive personal details (full ID numbers, etc.) before uploading if you prefer.

5. GCash & payments

BayaniHub does not process or hold money. All donations are sent directly via GCash to the number shown on the campaign (managed by the admin). You are responsible for sending the correct amount and reference. Admin verifies the transfer before releasing funds to the beneficiary. We never ask for your GCash PIN, OTP, or full account password.

6. Data storage

Currently this demo stores data in your browser’s localStorage (on the device you use). When the platform is upgraded to a real backend, data will be stored securely on servers located in or serving the Philippines, with access limited to authorized admins. You can clear local data anytime by clearing your browser storage.

7. Your rights

  • You may request deletion of your campaign or personal data by contacting the admin.
  • You may ask to see what information we hold about you.
  • You can withdraw a pending campaign before it is approved.

8. Children’s privacy

Campaigns involving minors should be created by a parent, guardian, or authorized adult. We do not knowingly collect personal data directly from children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, data requests, or concerns about a campaign, contact the BayaniHub admin through the channels published on the site or the email used for sponsor inquiries.
